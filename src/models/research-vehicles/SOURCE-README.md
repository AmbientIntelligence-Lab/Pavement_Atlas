# Generic research and assisted-driving vehicles

These original procedural models were authored for PaveData Atlas 1.3. They do not reproduce a particular manufacturer, university laboratory or production vehicle. All body dimensions, sensor locations, optical sketches and instrument counts are illustrative.

The research survey vehicle demonstrates a plausible combination of scene cameras, lidar, GNSS / inertial recording and downward-looking road profilers. The assisted-driving vehicle demonstrates a camera-and-radar configuration with a human driver responsible for driving. These are explanatory examples, not validated hardware specifications or autonomy classifications.

Dataset associations come from `data/vehicle-datasets.json`. A related dataset is not necessarily captured by the displayed vehicle. Read each relationship and limitation before reusing a dataset. Model annotations are not calibration or performance data.

The model can be exported as GLB from the sensor explorer. It uses metre-scale coordinates, +X forward, +Y up and +Z left. Materials and instrument metadata accompany the geometry where supported by the exporter.
