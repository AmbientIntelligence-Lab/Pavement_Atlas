Metadata that makes
a dataset useful.

A source link is the beginning. Acquisition context, annotation detail and reuse terms help researchers decide whether a dataset fits their work.

Submit one resource at a time. Required fields support source verification and a follow-up; optional fields make the eventual record easier to discover and compare.

Source and identity

Use the original authors' terminology. If a required fact cannot be established, enter Unknown for the year or license rather than guessing.

Collection, content and suitability

These fields help connect pavement data with sensing tasks and vehicle configurations. They describe evidence; they do not certify autonomous-driving readiness.

Domain metadata for pavement research

How review works

We check the source and the dataset's relevance, reconcile duplicates and versions, and assess the provided metadata. Missing details stay unknown until evidence supports them. We will contact you for a clarification or review outcome.

Vehicle associations in the Atlas are relevance aids. They do not imply that a vehicle manufacturer captured, uses or endorses a dataset.

Delivery and privacy

The form states its current delivery mode before submission. When automatic delivery is configured, a separate receipt page confirms mail-service acceptance. In email preparation mode, attach the generated Excel file and send it yourself.

Metadata and contact details are processed by the configured Google review service only when you submit through it, or by your email provider when you send the prepared email. Contact tianzhi.he@ami-lab.com about a contribution or deletion request.

Metadata organization is informed by the OpenConstruction schema reference and Schema.org Dataset. The Atlas adds pavement acquisition and review fields; this is a submission guide, not a claim of full schema equivalence.
