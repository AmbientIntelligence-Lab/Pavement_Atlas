Multi-criteria screening

Readiness screening, on your own weights.

Every dataset was screened against the same 40 checks (eight criteria × five) that the human review uses. Each check is Y confirmed by primary documentation, N failed, U unknown or X unresolved. Confirmed points count only Y; the upper bound adds every unresolved check. Move a slider or switch the weight profile and the ranking, the register and the gallery update. No final leaderboard is issued: every assessment is provisional until the human checks and the four eligibility gates are completed.

Weight profile
Eight criteria, each worth 0–5 confirmed checks. Points = Σ weight × confirmed / 5, normalised to 100. Drag a slider to build a custom profile.

Interactive simulation

Feel the road. Drive it.

A 3-D driving game seeded with the eight distress families in this inventory. Pick a vehicle, a scene, the weather and the time of day; switch between third-person, driver's seat and free orbit views and drag the mouse to look around. Headlights come on automatically at night, in rain and in fog and light the road ahead. Hitting a defect shakes the car, wears the suspension and shows a real reference photo of that distress in the corner — without blocking your view.

This atlas is a static page with no server, so a submission is packaged locally in your browser: send it by email (one click, pre-filled), or download the JSON and share it with the review team. Nothing is transmitted until you choose to send it. Submissions are also kept in your browser only below, so you can re-send or remove them. Review criteria mirror the inventory rules: real-world pavement or road-surface data, an image or image-bearing multimodal component, and a traceable access route.

PaveData Atlas 1.1 — generated from the reconciled inventory workbook (Compared_All_Platforms_TH_v5.0.xlsx, included in this repository). Records were compiled through a systematic multi-platform repository search, then deduplicated so each underlying dataset appears once; mirrors and duplicate deposits are merged with all links preserved. Missing values follow NR / NA / UNK / INACC coding. Where independent review records reported different values, both are preserved in the workbook and the dataset is flagged here. Camera-viewpoint labels (nadir / top-down versus forward-facing / oblique) were assigned from the acquisition metadata, the dataset page or the publication, each with a verbatim evidence quote, its source and a confidence; the weakest labels were re-checked by an independent second review (workbook sheet View_Orientation_Legend). Readiness screening uses eight criteria × five checks with Y / N / U / X states recorded by a provisional automated documentation review; confirmed points, upper bounds, evidence resolved and eligibility gates are recomputed on this page from the recorded check states (sheets MCDM_Rubric, MCDM_Settings, MCDM_Checks, MCDM_Grades). Human confirmation of the checks is pending, so no final leaderboard is issued.
Notes — Section illustrations and icons are stylized, embedded SVG; the sample gallery shows downscaled preview figures published by the dataset authors, embedded with attribution and links (all rights remain with the authors under each dataset's license). World map: Natural Earth country boundaries (public domain), equirectangular. The page works fully offline. Platform order: GitHub · Data.gov · Mendeley Data · Hugging Face · Zenodo · Kaggle. Inventory snapshot: August 2026; viewpoint labels and readiness screening: September 2026 (workbook v5.0). Screening values are provisional and recomputed on the page from the recorded check states.
