Camera viewpoint

Looking down at the surface, or ahead along the road?

Whether a dataset's photos look straight down at the pavement (nadir / top-down) or ahead along the road (forward-facing / oblique) decides which models, sensors and deployments they suit. Viewpoint is only classified when stated in the source. A vehicle mount alone does not establish camera direction. Open a record to inspect the evidence. Click a card to filter.

Multi-criteria screening

Compare the evidence behind the data.

The v4.0 rubric uses 16 checks across six dimensions. Select the labeled or unlabeled cohort, then adjust dimension weights. Confirmed evidence forms the lower bound; unknown and conflicting evidence widen the interval. Compare datasets within the same cohort. Scores describe documented evidence, not measured model performance.

Comparison cohort and weights
Each dimension is scaled by its own check count. Labeled-16 uses all six dimensions; Unlabeled-13 excludes label quality for the entire cohort. Custom weights are exploratory.
