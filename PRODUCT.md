# PaveData Atlas

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product purpose

A usable reference website for researchers and practitioners selecting pavement, road-surface and autonomous-driving datasets. Users find metadata, source access, evidence, limitations, and reproducible selection criteria, and explore acquisition through interactive vehicle and road simulations.

## Capabilities and constraints

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with locally bundled Three.js. Preserve original folders. Catalog claims must be traceable to source records. Vehicle models and road response are educational approximations, not verified manufacturer configurations or calibrated traffic/dynamics models. Community contributions require a name and email. The designated review recipient is tianzhi.he@ami-lab.com. Automatic email needs a separately configured server; the static fallback must identify that no message has been sent.
